Breakpoint Syntax

Add a breakpoint prefix before a grid span utility to change the span at that breakpoint and below. e.g. md:mds-grid-span-4 will apply a 3 column span at the medium breakpoint (768px) and below, while keeping the default span at larger breakpoints.

xl: @media (max-width: 1280px)
lg: @media (max-width: 1024px)
md: @media (max-width: 768px)
sm: @media (max-width: 480px)
xs: @media (max-width: 320px)
