Breakpoint Syntax

Add a breakpoint prefix before a grid span utility to change the span at that breakpoint and below.

Prefix Media Query Total Columns
xl: @media (max-width: 1280px) 12
lg: @media (max-width: 1024px) 12
md: @media (max-width: 768px) 6
sm: @media (max-width: 480px) 6
xs: @media (max-width: 320px) 6

Note: At md breakpoint, the total columns of the .mds-grid changes from 12 to 6. mds-grid-span-6 is the max column span, and will be displayed at full width. Column spans should not exceed total columns spans at all breakpoints.

Responsive Grid Utilities

Combine multiple breakpoint prefixes with mds-grid-span-* to create a responsive grid layout that changes across breakpoints. In this example, the grid items will be 6 columns wide by default, 4 columns wide at the lg breakpoint and below, 3 columns wide at the md breakpoint and below, and full width at the sm breakpoint and below.

.mds-grid-span-2 12 / 2 = 6 columns by default lg:mds-grid-span-3 12 / 3 = 4 columns at lg md:mds-grid-span-2 6 / 2 = 3 columns at md sm:mds-grid-span-6 6 / 6 = 1 column at sm
