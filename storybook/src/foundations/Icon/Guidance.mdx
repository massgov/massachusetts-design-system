# Icons guidance

---

## Content

### Best practices

Use icons from the approved [design system icon set](?path=/docs/foundations-icon--overview) whenever possible. Approved icons help create a consistent experience across Commonwealth digital products and reduce the need for teams to create or source their own symbols.

When choosing an icon, select the clearest and most familiar option for the action or meaning. Use the same icon for the same concept across experiences.

If an approved icon does not meet your needs, consider whether the interface needs an icon at all. New icons should be added only when they support a clear user need and can be reused across products or services.

If there is an icon missing from the library that you would like to include, please reach out to the design system team to suggest the addition. You can reach us at [designsystem@mass.gov](mailto:designsystem@mass.gov).

---

## Design

### When to use

Use icons when they help people quickly understand an action, status, category, or piece of content.

Icons are most helpful when they:

- Reinforce the meaning of nearby text.
- Help people scan a page or interface.
- Represent familiar actions, such as search, print, download, or close.
- Communicate common status messages, such as success, warning, error, or information.
- Support navigation patterns that people are likely to recognize.

Use icons intentionally. An icon should make the experience clearer, not just more visually decorated.

### When not to use

Do not use icons when they do not add meaning or when they make the interface harder to understand.

Avoid icons when:

- The text label is already clear on its own.
- The icon could be interpreted in multiple ways.
- The icon is unfamiliar to most people.
- The icon adds visual clutter.
- Multiple icons appear close together and become hard to scan.
- The icon is being used only for decoration.

---

## Accessibility

If an icon is embedded within a component, they must be accessible to people using assistive technology, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, or other tools.
