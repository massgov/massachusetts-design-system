# State Seal

This official emblem symbolizes the state's history and values, and provides a visual representation of Massachusetts' identity. The seal is used to validate official state documents, mark government communications, and serve as a symbol of state authority on official correspondence, buildings, and materials.

## Usage

Use buttons to initiate actions, submit forms, save changes, trigger processes, and support primary user tasks. Buttons are also appropriate for destructive actions that require confirmation, such as delete, remove, or cancel.

### When To Use

- On official state website global header and footer components
- Official legal documents, press releases, and communications
- Official presentations

### When Not To Use

- Unofficial content that is not explicitly authorized by the state government
- Political campaigns
- Social media profiles, unless it is an official government account
