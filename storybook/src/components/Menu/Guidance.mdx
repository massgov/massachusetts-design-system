# Menu guidance

---

## Content

### Best practices

#### Do

- Keep labels concise, ideally using only 1 to 3 words and fewer than 20 characters.
- Use plain language.
- Use sentence case.

#### Don't

- Use acronyms that may not be immediately understood.

---

## Design

### Usage

#### When to use a menu

- To provide a compact list of related actions or commands.
- When the actions are secondary and do not need to be visible at all times.
- When displaying all actions on the page would add unnecessary clutter.
- For familiar, predictable groups of actions, such as account or profile actions.

#### When not to use a menu

- For important or frequently used actions that should remain visible.
- When people need to see or compare all available options before making a choice.
- For navigation that would be clearer as visible links or other navigation components.
- As a form control for selecting and submitting a value; use a native selection component instead.

#### Responsive behavior

- Position the menu relative to its trigger and keep it within the visible viewport.
- On larger screens, allow the menu to use more available width if labels are wrapping to a second line.
- Avoid horizontal scrolling or truncating important menu labels.

---

## Accessibility

### Mouse control

- The menu opens on click.
- Select an option with a single click.
- Clicking outside the menu area closes it.

### Touch control

- Tap the menu trigger to open the menu.
- Tap outside the menu area to close it.
- Support momentum scrolling in longer lists.

### Keyboard control

- The Tab key navigates to the menu control.
- The Enter or Space key opens the menu when focused.
- The Up and Down arrow keys navigate between options.
- The Home and End keys move to the first and last options.
- Support type-ahead to quickly navigate to options starting with typed characters.
- The Enter key selects the focused option and closes the menu for single-select menus.
- The Escape key closes the menu without changing the selection.
- The Tab key moves focus out of an open menu and closes it.

### Assistive technology

- Add `role="menu"` to the menu container.
- Add `role="menuitem"` to each menu option.
- Provide an accessible label, such as `aria-label="User menu"` or `aria-label="Profile menu"`.
- Use `aria-haspopup="true"` and toggle `aria-expanded="true"` or `aria-expanded="false"` based on the menu state.
- Connect the trigger to the menu with `aria-controls="menu-id"`.
- Associate the menu with its trigger using `aria-labelledby="trigger-button-id"`.
- Announce when the menu opens, for example "menu expanded, 5 items," and when it closes.
- Announce the current item as people move through menu items, for example "Preferences, 1 of 5."
