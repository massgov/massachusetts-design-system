# Details toggle guidance

---

## Content

### Best practices

#### Do

- Write descriptive text labels that tell people what information they can reveal.
- Keep supporting content concise and focused on the task.
- Use plain language to explain unfamiliar terms or provide additional context.
- Keep instructions and information required to complete a task visible without expanding the toggle.

#### Don't

- Use vague labels that do not describe the supporting information.
- Repeat information already provided in visible labels or helper text.
- Hide required instructions, warnings, or other critical information inside the toggle.
- Include actions the user may need to take within the supporting content.

---

## Design

### When to use

- When people may benefit from supporting explanations, definitions, or legal requirements that do not need to be persistently visible.
- To reduce cognitive load for information display.

### When not to use

- For information people must read to complete a task.
- To hide required field instructions or validation messages.
- For long sections of content that would be clearer on a separate page or would be better suited to an accordion.

---

## Accessibility

### Mouse control

- People can click the details toggle text label to expand or collapse the supporting content.
- The pointer changes to indicate that the toggle is interactive.

### Touch control

- The toggle provides a minimum touch target of 44 by 44 pixels.
- The touch target includes the visible label and any padding needed to meet the minimum size.

### Keyboard control

- People can move focus to the toggle using Tab.
- People can expand or collapse the supporting content using Enter or Space.
- When expanded, focus remains on the toggle so people can continue navigating to the revealed content.

### Assistive technology

- Screen readers announce the toggle label and whether the supporting content is expanded or collapsed.
- People can expand or collapse the supporting content using assistive technology.
- The expanded content follows the toggle in reading order.
