# Badge overview

Badges display brief information, such as a status, count, or category. They help people identify information at a glance without requiring interaction.

Badges use color and text to distinguish different types of information. Choose the type based on the meaning of the label.

- `Neutral` identifies a category or status without emphasizing importance or urgency
- `Informative` draws attention to information or a status that people should notice, without indicating success or a problem
- `Success` communicates a completed or positive outcome
- `Warning` identifies a condition that needs attention but is not critical
- `Danger` identifies an error, critical condition, or serious problem
