# Footer overview

A footer is a block of information at the bottom of a webpage or application. It gives people a consistent place to find information about the site or service, contact information, supporting navigation, and required legal or policy links.

The footer combines required elements with optional navigation, contact, branding, and supporting content.

Use the footer to provide supporting information and secondary navigation after the main page content.

<FooterExample />

---

## Examples

### Mass.gov

The Mass.gov configuration uses Mass.gov as the site name and follows the standard footer structure used across Mass.gov pages.

<FooterExample siteName="Mass.gov" />

### Mass.gov subdomains

A Mass.gov subdomain uses a Mass.gov web address but is managed outside the Mass.gov Content Management System.

Use the service, organization, or product name as the site name when the footer represents a Mass.gov subdomain.

<FooterExample siteName="Example Mass.gov service" />

### Organization branding

The footer can include an organization or product logo next to the State Seal when the experience represents a specific organization or product.

Use the organization or product name as the site name when a name is included.

<FooterExample siteName="Organization or product name" />

---

## Required elements

### State Seal

Include the State Seal in every footer. It can appear with a site, service, organization, or product name, or on its own.

### Footer utility navigation bar

Include the Digital Accessibility Statement and Privacy Notice as the first links in the footer utility navigation bar. Add other required legal or policy links as needed.

### Copyright information

Include the copyright statement and Commonwealth trademark notice at the bottom of the footer.

---

## Optional elements

The footer can also include:

- The site, service, organization, or product name
- Organization or product description
- Social media links
- Contact information
- Supporting navigation links grouped under headings
- Additional legal, policy, or privacy links
- Additional supporting content
